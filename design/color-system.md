# Color system: concert hall

| Token | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Concert Black | #070708 | Page and canvas |
| Velvet Burgundy | #691E33 | Seats, speaker units, active scenario |
| Brass | #C59A49 | Rules, section labels, selected unit |
| Stage Amber | #F1A341 | Stage lights, primary actions, direct wavefront, high end of the heat map |
| Acoustic Blue | #3A80BF | Reflections, listener marker, low end of the heat map |
| Warm White | #F5E8D2 | Text |

Lighting, fog, the wavefront shader and the heat map use the same six colours; the heat ramp runs Acoustic Blue to Brass to Stage Amber.
