# Motion spec

- The wavefront is a radius, not a video: one uniform drives the reveal of every hall material, from the source and its six wall images.
- The audio level (Web Audio analyser) drives the stage lights' intensity and a ripple ring on the stage.
- Rays reflect and dim by each wall's 1 kHz absorption and are timed by path length over the speed of sound (slowed for the eye).
- Dragging a speaker re-predicts the field in chunks, so the map fills in progressively.
- Camera moves between audience, stage and ceiling use the same rig as the intro's hand-off.
- Waveforms are the section dividers and the axes of the energy chart.
- Ambient motion stops under Pause Motion and reduced motion.
